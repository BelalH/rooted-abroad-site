Privacy & data handling

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This page says exactly what happens to personal data on this site. It is deliberately specific rather than generic, including where we are still early-stage and cannot yet make a commitment. If anything here is unclear, email hello@kafupeople.com and ask.

Who is behind this site

Rooted Abroad is an early-stage programme being built by a small founding team. We are not yet in a position to publish a registered company name, company number, or registered address for it. Rather than name an entity we cannot stand behind, we have left it out and said so here.

Correspondence about Rooted Abroad is handled by that team from the mailbox hello@kafupeople.com, which is operated under the Kafu People name. The rootedabroad.com domain is not under our control and mail sent to it does not reach us.

For HR, global mobility and procurement reviewers: if you need to know precisely which legal entity you would be contracting with, who the data controller would be, or whether we can sign your data processing agreement, email us and we will answer honestly — including where the answer today is "not yet". We would rather lose a deal on that answer than give you a name you cannot verify. This page will be updated to name the entity, and its registered details, once there is one.

What we collect

1. If you fill in the form on the home page

The form asks for:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Which best describes you — accompanying partner, HR / global mobility, or something else
  • Employer & relocation context — a free-text box. Whatever you type here, we get. Please keep it to what is needed to have a first conversation.

Alongside that, the page includes the address of the page that referred you and any tracking parameters in the link you arrived on (for example ?utm_source=…), so we can tell where enquiries come from.

Right now the form has no backend of its own, so submitting it sends nothing anywhere. Your details stay in your browser, and the page offers you a pre-filled email — or a copyable block of text — addressed to hello@kafupeople.com. Nothing reaches us unless you choose to send that email yourself. If you close the tab instead, we never see it.

We are building a direct submission path for the form, hosted on Cloudflare Workers with the database created in the European Union. When it is switched on, submitting the form will send the fields above to that database directly, together with the country Cloudflare infers from your connection and your browser's user-agent string. This page will be updated to describe that as live rather than planned at the same time the form starts using it.

2. If you email us

We hold your message and your email address in our mailbox, along with anything else you chose to put in the email, for as long as described under retention below.

3. When anyone loads the page

We count traffic with a plain hit counter (hits.sh). It increments integers — total page views, and separate counts for the referral source, desktop versus mobile, and whether a form was started or submitted. No cookies are set, no identifier is stored on your device, and nothing we receive back distinguishes one visitor from another or follows you to other sites. We do not know who you are from this.

Two things are honest to add. First, counting is done by requesting an image from hits.sh, so hits.sh receives your IP address and user agent as part of serving that request, as any web server does — we only ever see the totals. Second, this site is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub processes request data, including IP addresses, as part of hosting it.

Why we use it, and on what lawful basis

DataPurposeLawful basis (UK/EU GDPR Art. 6)
Name, email, role, relocation context To reply to your enquiry and talk to you about the programme Legitimate interests — you contacted us and expect a reply (Art. 6(1)(f))
Referrer and campaign parameters To understand which channels bring enquiries Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f))
Anonymous page counts To see whether the page is being read at all Legitimate interests; no personal data is received by us (Art. 6(1)(f))

We do not use your details for anything else. No newsletter, no advertising, no profiling, no automated decision-making, and no selling or sharing of enquiry data with anyone. If we ever want to use your details for something beyond answering you, we will ask first.

If you are submitting someone else's details

Employers sometimes enquire on behalf of an employee's partner. If you do that, please send only the minimum needed to arrange a first conversation, tell the person you have done it, and point them at this page. We would rather have a sparse enquiry from you and talk to the partner directly than hold family-members' personal data we have no basis to hold. We will treat any such details as covered by this page and delete them on request from either of you.

The programme is for adults. Please do not send us information about children through this site.

Who else sees it

  • GitHub — hosts the site (GitHub Pages).
  • hits.sh — the anonymous hit counter described above.
  • Our email provider — handles the hello@kafupeople.com mailbox and therefore holds any email you send us.
  • Cloudflare — will host the form's database in the EU once that path is live. Not in use yet.

That is the complete list. No analytics suite, no advertising network, no CRM, no third-party embeds or fonts loaded from elsewhere. Some of these providers are outside the UK/EU, in which case transfers rely on their standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries we do not take forward: deleted within 12 months of our last exchange with you.
  • Enquiries that become an ongoing conversation: kept while that is live, then deleted within 24 months of the last contact.
  • Anything, at any time, if you ask: deleted within 30 days.
  • Anonymous page counts: kept indefinitely. They are integers and contain nothing about you.

Your rights, and how to use them

You can ask us to give you a copy of what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to what we are doing with it, or send it to you in a portable form. There is no charge and you do not need to explain why.

Email hello@kafupeople.com with "privacy request" in the subject. We will reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm you control the email address in question before we act, so that someone cannot delete or extract your data by asking in your name.

We have not appointed a data protection officer; we are too small to be required to. If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you are entitled to complain to your national data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). We would appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Security, stated plainly

Access to enquiry emails is limited to the founding team. The site is served over HTTPS. The planned form database will be in the EU with access limited to the same people. We are a small team using mainstream hosted services, and we are not going to claim a certification we do not hold — we have no ISO 27001, no SOC 2, and no penetration test to show you. If your vendor review needs those, say so early and we will tell you where we actually stand.

Changes to this page

When this changes materially — a named legal entity, the form's database going live, a new provider — we will update the date at the top and say what changed. If you have an open enquiry with us when something material changes, we will tell you.
